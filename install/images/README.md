Copy every PNG in this folder into your inventory images directory. Names match the item names.

- ox_inventory: `ox_inventory/web/images/`
- qb-inventory: `qb-inventory/html/images/`
- qs-inventory: `qs-inventory/html/images/` (or your configured image path)

Crowns, halos, and plushies that were already in this resource keep their existing icons. Wings, auras, shoulder pets, and `halo_orange` use a simple labeled icon until you replace the PNG with your own art.

Restart the inventory resource after copying. These are icons only — the 3D models still come from your existing stream resources.
